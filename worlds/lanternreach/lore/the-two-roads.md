# The Two Roads

People in Lanternreach say there are two roads to anywhere.

**The Long Road** is the Old Road: slow, honest, and open to everyone. The Lamplighters and the Wardens
walk it. So does anyone who keeps their promises when it would be easier not to.

**The Quick Way** is any road that skips the walking: a fox's door, a forged token, a favor owed. The
Bargain Fox and the Gilded Ledger know all the quick ways. They really are quicker.

Nobody in Lanternreach agrees about which road is best. The old stories have opinions, though.
