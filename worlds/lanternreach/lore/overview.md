# Lanternreach

Lanternreach is a land of villages, forests, marshes and mountains, joined by the Old Road. Every village
has a Hearth-Lantern in its square, lit with flame carried from the Ember: a warm star-coal kept in the
First Forge on top of Mount Cinder.

As long as the lanterns burn, the Gloam Host stays in the dark places. When a lantern goes out, the
shadows creep in.

Every spring, the Lamplighters' Guild carries new flame along the Old Road, on foot, to every village,
however small. It is slow. It is how it has always been done properly.
