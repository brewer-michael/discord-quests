# The Ember

The Ember is a coal of starlight that fell onto Mount Cinder long ago. The first Lamplighters built the
First Forge around it, and learned that its flame could be shared without ever running out, as long as
it was shared freely.

The Ember does not like to be kept. Held too long by one person, it grows heavy and cold, and it begins
to whisper that it wants to stay. The First Forge only takes it back from a hand that lets it go.

The Gloam King stole the Ember to keep for himself. He has been cold ever since.
